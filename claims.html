How claims work

Three steps, from the vet to the portal.

Timing and outcomes depend on the policy terms and the details of each claim.

  1. 01

    Step 1: Visit Your Veterinarian

    Get the care your pet needs. Keep relevant invoices and treatment documents.

    Keep: invoice, treatment notes

  2. 02

    Step 2: Submit Claim Information

    Provide the required details and supporting documents through the claim-submission process.

    Provide: claim details, documents

  3. 03

    Step 3: Track Claim Progress

    Review the current status and any next steps shown in the portal.

    Review: status, next steps

Prepare your documents

What a claim may ask for.

  • Policy or member reference
  • Pet and treatment details · see ① on the sample
  • Veterinary invoice · itemised, see ② and ③
  • Supporting medical records · where required
  • Payment or reimbursement details · where applicable

Exact requirements depend on the policy and the nature of the claim. This prototype never asks for real financial or medical information.
