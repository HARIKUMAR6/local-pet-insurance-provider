Before you choose

Cover works best when it starts early.

Conditions that show signs before cover begins, or during a waiting period, are usually excluded. Insuring a young, healthy pet keeps more of their future care eligible, so check each plan's waiting periods and exclusions first.

Unexpected vet bills

Plan for the visit you didn't see coming.

Accidents and sudden illness rarely arrive in a convenient month. Choose an annual limit that would cover a serious treatment and a deductible you could pay at short notice, and keep every itemised invoice for your claim.
