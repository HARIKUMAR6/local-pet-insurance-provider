Understand what matters

Four terms worth comparing.

Annual LimitThe maximum eligible amount payable within the policy period, subject to policy terms.
ReimbursementThe eligible share of covered expenses paid back according to the policy.
DeductibleThe amount you may need to pay before reimbursement applies.
Optional BenefitsAdditional coverage features that may be available, depending on the policy.

Before you choose

Choose With Clear Expectations

No policy covers every veterinary expense. Check these points in the policy terms first.

  • Exclusions · which conditions and treatments are not covered.
  • Waiting periods · when cover begins after you buy.
  • Reimbursement rules · how eligible costs are calculated and paid back.
  • Annual limits · the most a policy pays in a policy period.
  • Eligibility · any age, species or health requirements.
