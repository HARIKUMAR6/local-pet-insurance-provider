About Pawstead

Protection for the Pets Who Make Life Better.

Explore Our Plans

Why Pawstead exists

Care Comes First. Clarity Matters.

A visit to the vet can bring decisions nobody planned for, often at short notice and with costs that are hard to predict.

Knowing what your cover includes before that moment helps you focus on your pet, not the paperwork.

Understanding cover early makes choices at the vet clearer.

Our approach

Our Approach to Pet Insurance

Three principles shape how we present cover, from the first comparison to the last claim.

  1. 01

    Clear Information

    Help pet owners understand coverage options and the policy details that matter most.

  2. 02

    Practical Guidance

    Make it easier to explore plans side by side and to understand how the claim process works.

  3. 03

    Responsible Expectations

    Encourage owners to review exclusions, limits, deductibles and policy conditions before choosing cover.
